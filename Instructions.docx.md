Poster Design and Printing

* Review the poster resources folder. You can find templates, logos, etc. in the folder.  
* The dimensions of the poster must not exceed 42”x 60”. **Preferred poster size: 42x56**  
* Files cannot be larger than 40MB.  
* Save your file as: Last Name\_TACC\_Poster Size  
  * Example: Gomez\_TACC\_42x56  
* Your file(s) must be uploaded in PDF format in the Final Posters folder no later than **Monday, July 27 at 9am CST. This is the final deadline.** If your poster is not uploaded at this time, you will be responsible for arranging and incurring the cost of printing. 

Mock presentations: [Sign up](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1KvTHBtZm9rtp1q0NzsnYn-IVJYyxNObv/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=114534384258358105055&rtpof=true&sd=true) for a mock poster presentation time.

1. We are expecting a brief overview of your research project. Do not exceed 5 minutes. You will be timed.   
2. A poster presentation is   
   1. Interactive  
   2. A conversation  
   3. A concise overview of your research project  
   4. Supported by your discussion with the audience (we will ask you Qs)  
3. “Posters should not attempt to replace a research paper” \- do not read from your research poster  
4. Review Key Content Strategies \- SRS Poster Workshop Slides (see professional development folder). Directly from slides:  
   1. Consider your target audience (general audience)  
   2. Emphasize the relevance/significance of the project (societal impact)  
   3. Highlight future directions  
   4. Limit yourself to a few key analyses/data points to make your case  
   5. Provide enough details that someone can understand if you’re not present to talk them through, but don’t go overboard  
5. Your introduction includes:  
   1. Full Name  
   2. Major and Institution  
   3. Mention REU Program and Mentors  
   4. Research project  
6. Acknowledgments \- thank the National Science Foundation, The University of Texas at Austin Texas Advanced Computing Center, and cite the NSF REU Site: Cyberinfrastructure Research for Societal Advancement, Award \# 2447887\. **You may reference recent REU poster examples (found in the shared folder).**
