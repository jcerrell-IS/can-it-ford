`WATER_SIZE` and `WATER_POS` **do not exist as named constants.** The water morph is

```
149:            pos=(-1.8, 0.0, water_depth / 2.0),

```

**Domain and dx.** Domain is `7.0 x 2.0 x 2.6 m`. The script's own formula is
`dx_cell = 1.0 / grid_density` (seen at line 267 of the sibling retest script), giving
`dx = 0.015625 m` at `grid_density=64` and `0.0078125 m` at 128. Genesis pads inward by
`3*dx` per side: `0.0469 m/side` at 64, `0.0234 m/side` at 128.

**Water layer count: 30 at gd=64, 60 at gd=128.** Derived from measured particle counts,
not from the dx formula: water volume `0.35 * 1.8 * 0.30 = 0.189 m3`, and the crash-isolation
run reports 189,000 particles at gd=64, so volume per particle is exactly `1.0e-6 m3`, i.e.
`0.01 m` spacing, i.e. `0.30 / 0.01 = 30` layers.

**Discrepancy worth flagging, not papered over.** Empirical particle spacing is
`0.64 / grid_density` (0.01 m at 64, 0.005 m at 128), which is **not** `dx_cell = 1/grid_density`.
Spacing is 0.64x the cell size. Whether that is deliberate sub-cell sampling or a mismatch
is unresolved, and it bears directly on the A9 fill-fraction question.

**Ledger F4 status: STILL LIVE, with one correction to the ledger's own arithmetic.**

`VEHICLE_SIZE` at line 26 is still the stale `(4.66, 1.79, 1.44)`. Box volume

Independent corroboration from Vista's own crash-isolation document

script is on the old parameterization. Fine for crash isolation, wrong for physics."
