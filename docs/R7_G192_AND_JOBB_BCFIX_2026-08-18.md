### The `sustain_frames` fragility is gone at both g160 and g192

`sustain_frames = 3` at `failure_modes.py:52` is unsourced and, at g128, a one-integer change
flips all six draws. That fragility **cannot** apply at g160 or g192: the joint slide condition
holds for **zero** frames in all ten draws across the two grids, so the verdict is STUCK for
any `sustain_frames >= 1`. Under-resolved gives a fragile SLIDE that an arbitrary integer can
flip; resolved gives a STUCK that no choice of that integer can move. That is a coherent
picture and it is worth stating as such.

### THE CONFOUND IS NOT RESOLVED AND g192 INHERITS IT, AND MAKES IT WORSE

`grid_lim` is **identical at 9.42174 m on all six grids**, which is exactly why this trap is
easy to fall into. But the driver sets `wall = 4*dx` on each side, so the usable water span is
`grid_lim * (1 - 8/n_grid)` and dx shrinks with n:

| grid | span (m) | vs g48 |
|---|---|---|
| g48 | 7.8515 | 1.0000x |
| g64 | 8.2440 | 1.0500x |
| g96 | 8.6366 | 1.1000x |
| g128 | 8.8329 | 1.1250x |
| g160 | 8.9507 | 1.1400x |
| **g192** | **9.0292** | **1.1500x** |

**The tank is LARGEST exactly where the verdict is STUCK.** Refinement and tank enlargement are
co-directional across the entire ladder, and adding g192 extends the confounded range rather
than breaking it: g192 is both the finest grid and the biggest tank. This run cannot separate
the two, and neither can any other run in the ladder.

**Report this as "the verdict flips under a refinement that also enlarges the tank", never as
"the verdict flips under refinement".** The pinned-span control
(`scripts/pinned_span_wrapper.py`, handoff item 1) remains the highest-priority experiment in
the project, and g192 raises its value rather than reducing it: there are now two STUCK grids
to explain, not one.
