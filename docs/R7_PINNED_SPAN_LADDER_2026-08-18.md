## 1. RESULT

**The flip survives the pinned span.** But the honest statement is stronger and narrower
than "the flip survives", and it comes with a gate breach that must travel with it.

1. **The tank effect is measured, not inferred, and it is small and of the opposite sign.**
   Increasing water volume by **+32.94 percent** at identical `dx`, `h`, realized depth,
   `n_vehicle` and `solid_volume_m3` changes max surge drift by **+3.54 percent**, and it
   changes it toward **SLIDE**. Resolution changes the same quantity by a factor of
   **5.14**. The two effects are two orders of magnitude apart.
2. **The premise that tank growth pushes toward STUCK is refuted by this project's own
   data.** A bigger tank gives *more* drift. Tank growth was working *against* the flip,
   not producing it. So the confound was real but it was **conservative**: correcting it
   makes the resolution finding stronger.
3. **The verdict transition is threshold-fragile; the continuous measure is not.** At
   constant pinned depth, max surge drift falls monotonically with resolution:
   0.18276, 0.08659, 0.06335, 0.04629, 0.03552 m at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 layers.
4. **8 of 10 pinned rungs fail the project's own P-2 containment gate; 0 of 6 unpinned
   rungs do.** Every pinned STUCK rung is a P-2 failure. See section 5.

## 2. THE CONFOUND

`sim_standing.py` sets the domain from the hull alone:

```
lim = max(2.2*ext[1], 3.5*ext[0], 6.0*depth)      # independent of n_grid
```

while the offsets are set **in cells**, `floor = 3.0*dx` and `wall = 4.0*dx`. So the water
interior span is `lim - 8*dx = lim*(1 - 8/n)` and it **grows** under refinement.
Recomputed live from the six unpinned summaries, g48 to g128: span **+12.50 percent**,
plan area **+26.56 percent**, water volume **+30.69 percent**; across the full six-rung
unpinned ladder the water-volume spread is **+36.94 percent**.

The confound is a property of **both** driver copies, at `:82`/`:86`/`:100` in the as-ran
file and `:160`/`:164`/`:178` in the canonical one.

## 3. METHOD

`sim_standing.py` was **not edited**. The span is pinned by choosing `lim` per grid,
`lim_n = S*n/(n-8)`, reached by presenting `extent[1] = lim_n/2.2`, monkeypatched inside
`canonicalize()` by `scripts/pinned_span_wrapper.py`. `S = 7.851451928106448 m`, the g48
interior span. `canonicalize()` is called at exactly two sites (`:234`, `:235`), both
module-global, so the override reaches `:81` (scene `lim`), `:236` (`h_probe`),
`:239`/`:240` (determinism probe) and `:337`.

Achieved: span pinned to **8.9e-16 m** max error on all 10 rungs.

### 3.2 Two variants

Pinning the span does **not** pin the realized water depth: depth is quantized to `L*h`
with `L = ceil(depth/h - 0.5)`, so it moves unless `40 | (n-8)`.

| variant | pinned | grids |
|---|---|---|
| **free** | span only | 48, 64, 96, 128, 141, 160, 192 |
| **exact** | span **and** realized depth | 48, 88, 128, 168, 208 |

`n = 48` and `n = 128` are exact-depth grids, run **once** and shared. The
`--require-exact-depth` guard was confirmed to actually refuse (at `n = 96`, "+6.061% off").

Water-volume spread: unpinned **+36.94 %**, pinned free **+8.90 %**, pinned exact
**+3.00 %**.

**`n = 141` was added** because at the pinned span 10 layers falls at `n` in 133 to 145,
not at 160: the smaller domain buys a finer `h` at the same `n`. The prescribed ladder
brackets 10 layers but never hits it. `n = 141` gives exactly 10 layers at +0.251 percent
depth deviation. An addition, not a substitution.

### 3.3 The real hull falsifier

**`hull_m3` cannot falsify anything and the first draft was wrong to use it.** It is a
hardcoded literal, `sim_standing.py:15` `HULL = 3.542739`, written verbatim to the summary
at `:360`. It prints 3.542739 whatever the hull is.

The real falsifier is the **matched-`h` identity**. Three pairs exist where `dx`, `h` and
realized depth coincide between the two ladders:

| layers | pair | `n_vehicle` | `parity_total_columns` | `solid_volume_m3` rel diff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | unp g48 / pin g48 | 3846 = 3846 | 754 = 754 | 0.0 |
| 6 | unp g96 / pin g88 | 29804 = 29804 | 2981 = 2981 | 3.8e-16 |
| 12 | unp g192 / pin g168 | 240127 = 240127 | 11894 = 11894 | 3.8e-16 |

`n_vehicle` and `parity_total_columns` are integers **measured from the particle cloud and
the raw mesh bbox**, and they match exactly across a presented-`ext_y` difference of up to
12.5 percent. That is proof the hull is untouched. (`h` agrees to about 1 ULP rather than
bitwise, because `lim_pinned(88) = LIM*11/12` gives `dx = LIM/96` by a different float
path. An earlier strict `==` in the analysis reported this as BROKEN and was my error.)

### 3.4 Null control

At `n = 48` the pinned `lim` equals the unpinned `lim`, so the patch is a **no-op** and the
rung must reproduce job 918250. It does, on all 13 compared fields. Note the limit: being a
no-op, it cannot detect a bug that only fires when `ext_y` actually changes. Section 3.3
covers that case.

### 3.5 Classifier

`simulation/failure_modes.py`, `G = 9.81`, `ssf = 1.42`, `slide_m = 0.05 m`,
`slide_speed_ms = 0.05 m/s`, `sustain_frames = 3`. Identical code path for both ladders.
No truncation: every run is `--frames 90`, 91 data rows at indices 0 to 90, last `t = 3.0`
s, which *is* the canonical horizon. Validated before use: it reproduces the published
unpinned table exactly.

`ssf = 1.42` is `compact_sedan`, applied to a 2337 kg body that falls inside
`light_pickup` (ssf 1.19). Re-run at 1.42, 1.19 and 1.04, **no verdict changes** at
`sustain_frames = 3`.

### 3.6 Settle

`settle_frames = 8` (constructor default, as-ran `:76`; the scene is built at `:251-253`
without passing it), identical on **every rung of both ladders**, running before the
one-shot velocity kick. Settle sim time is 8/30 s = 0.26667 s, constant. Settle *substep*
count is not constant (64 at n=48 to 288 at pinned n=192). At `c = 12.845 m/s` one traverse
of the pinned span is 0.611 s, so the 90-frame window holds roughly 2.5 acoustic round
trips: **every run here is recorded inside a transient**.

### 4.1 The exact variant, span and depth both pinned

| layers | n | verdict (N=5) | margin | max drift (m) | frames drift >= 0.05 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 48 | SLIDE 5/5 | 8 | 0.18276 | 87 of 91 |
| 6 | 88 | SLIDE 5/5 | 0 to 1 | 0.08659 | 86 |
| 9 | 128 | **STUCK 5/5** | -3 to -2 | 0.06335 | 83 to 84 |
| 12 | 168 | STUCK 5/5 | -3 | 0.04629 | 0 |
| 15 | 208 | STUCK 5/5 | -3 | 0.03552 | 0 |

Monotone in max drift, a fall of **80.6 percent** (factor 5.14) from 3 to 15 layers.
On mean drift the same series fits **drift = 0.537 * layers^-0.998** with residuals within
+4.9 / -4.7 percent, i.e. drift is inversely proportional to resolution to within 5 percent
over a 5x range.

### 4.2 The tank effect, measured directly

| layers | tank change | max drift change |
|---|---|---|
| 3 (null) | +0.00 % | +0.05 % |
| 6 | +21.66 % | +0.42 % |
| 12 | +32.94 % | +3.54 % |

**A bigger tank gives more drift**, toward SLIDE. Tank growth opposed the flip.

### 4.3 The free variant is non-monotone, and the outlier is not fully explained

Margins run 3 layers 8, 4 layers 5, 6 layers 0 to 1, **7 layers 4**, 8 layers 0. The
7-layer rung (pinned n=96) sits **44.7 percent above** the constant-depth trend. Its
realized depth is +6.061 percent, the largest deviation in the set, which is directionally
consistent, but the largest depth elasticity measurable anywhere in this dataset (4.57, at
12 layers) accounts for only about 27.7 of the 44.7 points. **The point is not fully
explained**, and attributing it to depth alone would be explaining it away.

Related: the two 12-layer pinned rungs are **not** interchangeable. g160-free max drift
0.05765 with 78 to 82 frames over the drift criterion; g168-exact 0.04629 with **0**. Same
layer count, different physical state, because at fixed layers `h` and depth move together.

### 4.3a Why drift is used here at all, given CLAUDE.md item 5

Item 5 says to cite the binary verdict and **never** the displacement magnitude, because on
the unpinned ladder displacement moved non-monotonically (+87.8 then -59.2 percent). That
warning is about comparing magnitudes **across resolutions**, and it still stands:

- **Section 4.2 is safe from it.** Those are ratios at *identical* `dx`, `h` and depth, so
  no cross-resolution comparison is involved. That is what makes the tank bound quotable.
- **Section 4.1 is a cross-resolution series and is NOT converged.** Drift is still falling
  **23.3 percent** from 12 to 15 layers. It is used as the *diagnostic to steer by*, because
  the verdict is threshold-fragile (4.4), and it is monotone here with a per-rung
  coefficient of variation of **0.46 to 0.99 percent** (N=5, population sd, measured on the
  pinned exact rungs: mean 0.181101, 0.085607, 0.062866, 0.045701, 0.035039 m at 3, 6, 9,
  12, 15 layers). **It is not offered as a converged magnitude and must not be cited as
  one.**

### 4.4 What the transition actually is

- Within the pinned ladder the bracket is (7, 9] layers; within the unpinned, (8, 10].
- Combining them under a monotonicity assumption narrows it to **(8, 9]** layers. But
  monotonicity is exactly what 4.3 breaks, and the combination pairs runs whose tanks
  differ by 27.1 percent, which is only defensible because section 4.2 bounds that effect
  at 3.5 percent.
- **It is contingent on the unsourced `sustain_frames = 3`** (`failure_modes.py:52`). At
  `sustain_frames = 4` the transition moves to between 4 and 6 layers.

STUCK verdicts themselves are robust for any `sustain_frames >= 2`; at 1, pinned g128 is
4 STUCK / 1 SLIDE.

**`margin = -3` does not mean the vehicle stayed put.** At pinned g128, the 9-layer rung
that anchors the flip, drift exceeds the 0.05 m criterion for **83 of 91 frames**, peaking
26.7 percent over it. It is STUCK only because drift and speed never overlap for 3
consecutive frames.

## 6. WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT DOES NOT SEPARATE

- **`h`, not "flow-depth resolution".** At fixed depth, layers across the depth and
  particles across the 177.4 mm underbody clearance are **perfectly collinear** (ratio
  0.602521 on every exact rung), both being `const/h`. This separates tank size from `h`.
  It does not separate depth resolution from clearance resolution or hull resolution.
- **Vehicle placement.** `vx = 0.60*lim`, so upstream fetch is not pinned: it moves
  **-2.52 percent** across the pinned ladder against +12.10 percent unpinned. Smaller, but
  co-directional with STUCK, so it is a residual confound in the same direction.
- **Inflow-slab to hull gap.** `:145` sets `_inflow_x = wall + inflow_len` with
  `inflow_len = 1.5` m (`:77`), a **constant** offset, while the hull sits at `0.60*lim`.
  So the distance from the forcing slab to the hull is not pinned either. Unpinned it grows
  3.3679 to 3.9568 m, **+17.48 percent**; pinned it moves 3.3679 to 3.2450 m over the same
  n range, **-3.65 percent**. Reduced 4.8x and sign-reversed, not eliminated. Raised by the
  `r7-collect` session and re-derived here.
- **Initial contact gap.** `_place` z is `floor + 0.5*h`, so the hull starts 49.1 mm above
  the floor at n=48 and 9.8 mm at n=208, a 5x change.
- **Water block width.** `(2n-17)*h = S - h/2`, so the water block is a half-cell narrower
  than S and grows **+1.01 percent** across the ladder (against +16.14 percent unpinned).
  n=64 is the only rung where `np.arange` overshoots to 112 columns and lands exactly on S.
- **Seeds.** `seed = 0` at `:77`. Every setup field is bit-identical across the 5 repeats,
  so these are **5 numerical repeats** sampling GPU non-associativity, **not** an ensemble
  over initial conditions. `bit_distinct` shows outputs differ; it does not make them a
  sample.
- **The 10-layer convention.** CLAUDE.md L-3 records that **no accepted particle
  force-convergence criterion exists for MPM** and that 10 layers is a rule of thumb. The
  first draft called it "the only depth-based convention" and said the flip lands on it
  "exactly"; both are over-claims, and at pinned span the flip is at **9** layers.

## 7. SIDE EFFECT INTRODUCED BY THE WRAPPER

`:337` writes the patched `extent` to **`rollout.npz`**, not to `summary.json` as the first
draft said. So every pinned `rollout.npz` stores a hull extent up to 12.5 percent below the
true one. Any downstream consumer reading it as the hull extent gets a wrong number with no
warning. `summary.json` has no `extent` key and is unaffected.
